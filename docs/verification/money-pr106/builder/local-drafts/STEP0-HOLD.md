## Decisions for this HOLD

1. **Scope of upkeepBps.** Apply it at each scheduled building charge, including B1 Mansion and Retainers' Hall. Keep contractual wages and Farm batch inputs at their data/base rates. Confirm charging by tile so the affordability order stays deterministic. This interprets the plan's weekly upkeep total as the sum of rounded scheduled building charges. Aggregate rounding would need an allocation convention and can change which building receives funding.
2. **Approve the additional paths in item 9.** The composed candidate makes the world-factory, allocation and UI preview edits listed there. Decide whether the retune should preserve current base-data ownership in buildings.json and npc-generator.json or move the relevant values to tuning.json. The packet proposes retaining single-source existing catalogs with an explicit exception to the tuning-only rule if those values move.
3. **Approve the calibration interpretation in items 6–7.** Good uses production and food allocations within the actual workforce caps, with additional Makers housing when affordable. Bad records the first exact zero at End Week and reports post-card zero separately in the raw trace. A chosen tolerance, a post-card bankruptcy definition, or different workforce/food mechanics changes the acceptance criterion and needs approval.
4. **Approve scope of the final golden delta.** Plan M1.1 needs a v14 golden for its GREEN checks. A numeric retune that affects the scenario needs a second reviewed delta in M1.2 or an explicit sequencing change. The known shape-only proposal here makes M1.1 consistent. Retune-driven downstream crime, hunger, NPC, or acquisition changes require named paths and owner review before adoption. The same approval covers any explicit machine-readable IndexedDB UI refusal code, if required beyond the existing refusal message and the codec's INCOMPATIBLE_SAVE.
