## Findings

**F1 · P2 · Fitted leadership loses a milli of safe Money and can lose a basis point of fatal protection.** At `packages/core/src/mansion/effects.ts:146` and `:137`, the code floors the result of multiplying by the floating-point ratio returned at line 67. I loaded the supplied Normal v15 fixture, used paid level-2 Guard Quarters with a fitted Captain and one fitted Guard, removed the other Guard roles and used the shipped catalog. The resulting Planning state passed serialization. The Guard’s safe contribution should be 1,500 × 11,500 / 10,000 = **1,725 milli**. Production returned **1,724**. In a second valid state with 14 Guards, eight fitted, plus a fitted Captain, production returned **3,554 bps** of fatal reduction. The exact value is (14 + 8 × 0.5) × 150 × 1.15 + 450 = **3,555 bps**. At zero Combat reduction this changes the fatal threshold from 1,445 to 1,446 bps. Keep the calculation in integer units through the final floor and cover both results with shipped-value tests. Both read-only Node probes exited 0.

**F2 · P2 · A v15 renewal notice can retain the Captain seat through renewal.** `packages/core/src/mansion/rules.ts:16` and `:31` use only the week counter. Effects make the same choice at `packages/core/src/mansion/effects.ts:117`. In contrast, the notice command accepts Renew on the notice itself at `packages/simulation/src/household-week.ts:109` and carries the resulting role through the new contract at lines 115–120. I gave the fixture’s guild Captain a renewal notice with one stored week remaining. Both the production v15 writer and reader accepted it. Assigning another resident as Captain returned `ROLE_CAP`. Answering Renew succeeded with the incumbent still Captain and no fresh `SET_ROLE`. This is an accepted import with inconsistent fields. The ordinary zero-week expiry path behaves correctly. Give the pending notice consistent meaning across eligibility, benefits, cap counting and renewal, and exercise this accepted input through the codec. The round-trip/command probe exited 0.

**F3 · P2 · The performance gate can pass an unresolved Normal tail.** At `delivery/measure-mansion-week.mjs:165`, the candidate statistic is the lower median of eight paired block p95 differences. The control bound at line 161 is the **largest absolute** A/A difference. Each block contains eight dispatches, so nearest-rank p95 at line 110 is its maximum. The flag cannot establish the plan’s full-dispatch claim.

From `delivery/perf-final-head/raw-paired.ndjson`, Normal pooled p95 moved **82.7091 → 128.2242 ms** (+45.5151 ms). All six head samples of at least 100 ms occurred in that block. The paired median duration increased in seven of eight Normal blocks. That concentration makes causal attribution unsafe. It also explains why the +2.1882 ms median block p95 shift cannot clear the observed tail. Easy pooled p95 fell from 63.3076 to 49.5187 ms. The earlier runs in the delivery are on a different tree. Mark the Normal comparison inconclusive until a comparable paired tail statistic and matched control evidence resolve it. These calculations use existing raw observations only.

Step 0 line 48 calls for per-room amounts and factors in Homes, and R2.3 requires the upkeep breakdown. Expose the catalog-derived charge with the room identity and level and retain the total and its factors. Cover a household with two chargeable rooms.
