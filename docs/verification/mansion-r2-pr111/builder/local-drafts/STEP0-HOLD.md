3. **Define structural robbery bonuses and when they apply.** **A (recommended):** Paid Vault bonuses operate even with an empty Vault; the legacy exact-value control has zero active Mansion purse terms (starter rooms, no staff). 4. **End-of-contract role semantics.** **A (recommended):** End any of the five R2 staff roles at `weeksLeft === 0`, including a renewal offer. Keep the occupied room during renewal, and release the role cap immediately. Exclude expired servants from new role assignment until renewal. Renewing requires a fresh role assignment. **B:** Retain the role while the renewal offer is pending, and release the cap only on actual departure. This matches the old occupancy timeline but needs clarification against spec §6.1 (“or her servant contract ends”). `died` from a valid save represents an already absent resident, so it never consumes a slot under either option.
5. **Use v15's complete shape for the performance fixture.** **A (recommended):** Benchmark all eight valid role IDs and filled furniture slots using a synthetic Planning save accepted by both builds.
